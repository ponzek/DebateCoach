How It Works
Round 1
Debate your topic with the first AI partner - 5 back-and-forth exchanges. After your 5th, a button appears. Click it to continue.
Round 2
Same topic, different AI partner. Complete 5 exchanges, then click the button to move to the quick survey before Round 3.
Round 3
Final round with a third AI partner. After 5 exchanges click the button, complete the survey, then do a final comparison of all three rounds.

About this Study

A within-subjects study comparing three AI debate configurations. You'll debate the same topic with all three; order is counterbalanced across participants.

What you'll do: Three rounds (5 exchanges each), an 8-question survey after each round, then a final comparison with rankings and two written responses.

Time: ~25–30 minutes total.

Data collected: Transcripts, per-round ratings (1–5), comparative rankings, and two written responses. No personal info beyond your auto-assigned participant ID.
