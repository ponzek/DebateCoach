About this Study

This is a within-subjects pilot study comparing three AI debate configurations. You will debate the same topic with all three conditions (A, B, C) in sequence.

What you'll do: Each debate requires 5 exchanges. After all three, you'll complete a short survey rating each experience.

Time: Approximately 15-20 minutes total.

Data collected: Conversation transcripts and your survey responses (1-5 scale ratings). No personal information is stored beyond your auto-assigned participant ID.
