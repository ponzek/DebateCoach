📋 Study Conditions

A — Vanilla GPT-4o
No special instructions. Baseline behavior for comparison.
B — Prompted Devil's Advocate
Engineered system prompt guiding the AI to challenge constructively without straw-manning.
C — Adapted Model
Fine-tuned on high-quality debate transcripts for deeper, more principled challenges.

🔬 About this Study

This is a within-subjects pilot study — you'll complete all three conditions across separate sessions in counterbalanced order. After each debate, you'll rate stance reconsideration, fairness, helpfulness, and frustration on a 1–5 scale.
