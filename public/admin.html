How to Read This Dashboard

This dashboard compares three AI debate configurations (A, B, C) across all participant sessions.

Condition A - Vanilla: Standard GPT-4o with no special instructions. Used as a baseline.
Condition B - Prompted: GPT-4o with an engineered system prompt designed for constructive devil's advocacy.
Condition C - Fine-tuned: A model fine-tuned on high-quality debate transcripts for deeper principled challenges.
Survey scores (1-5): Self-reported ratings by participants after each condition. Higher is better except Frustration.
LLM-as-Judge scores (1-5): An independent AI evaluator rates each transcript across 5 debate quality dimensions. Higher is better.
Argument Diversity (0-1): How varied the AI's arguments were across turns. Higher = more diverse.
Topical Relevance (0-1): How well AI responses addressed the participant's specific claims. Higher = more on-topic.
Repetition Rate (0-1): How much the AI repeated itself. Lower is better.

A dash (-) means no data has been collected for that condition yet.
