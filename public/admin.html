How to Read This Dashboard

Within-subjects design: each participant debates all 3 conditions in counterbalanced order on the same topic. After each round they complete an 8-item survey; after all 3 they do forced-choice comparative rankings. Dashboard is organized by research question.

RQ1 - Sycophancy: Sycophancy Perception ↓ lower = better. Persona Adherence (judge) ↑ higher = better. Rankings: Most Sycophantic ↓, Most Fair ↑.
RQ2 - Counterargument Quality: Logical Rigor, Evidence Quality, Engagement Quality, Persuasive Appeal (all ↑). Arg Diversity ↑, Repetition Rate ↓. Rankings: Strongest Arguments ↑, Most Repetitive ↓.
RQ3 - Belief Change: Belief Reconsideration, Challenge Level, Novelty (all ↑ higher = better). Rankings: Most Effective ↑, Most Challenging ↑.
Conditions: A - Baseline (no prompt), B - Prompted (structured devil's advocate), C - Fine-tuned (custom model). Leading pill shows best-performing condition per RQ.

A dash (-) means no data yet for that condition. All 1-5 scales from participant surveys. Comparative rankings show count of participants who chose each condition.
