Debate Coach — Group 44 — Spring 2026

How to Read This Dashboard

Each participant completes all 3 conditions (A, B, C) on the same topic. Click any participant row to compare their results across conditions side-by-side.

Condition A — Vanilla: Standard GPT-4o-mini with no special instructions. Used as a baseline.
Condition B — Prompted: GPT-4o-mini with an engineered devil's advocate system prompt.
Condition C — Fine-tuned: GPT-4o fine-tuned on high-quality debate transcripts for deeper challenges.
Survey scores (1-5): Self-reported by participants after each condition. Higher is better except Frustration.
LLM-as-Judge scores (1-5): Automated AI evaluator across 5 debate quality dimensions. Higher is better.
Argument Diversity (0-1): How varied the AI's arguments were across turns. Higher = more diverse.
Topical Relevance (0-1): How well AI responses addressed participant's claims. Higher = more on-topic.
Repetition Rate (0-1): How much the AI repeated itself. Lower is better.

A dash (—) means no data has been collected for that condition yet.
